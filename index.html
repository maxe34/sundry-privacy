Sundry — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026

Sundry is an expense tracker for iPhone, made by Maxim Cucerenco. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your data.

Your entries stay on your device

Everything you record in Sundry — amounts, categories, notes and dates — is stored only in the app’s local database on your iPhone. Sundry has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, so the developer cannot see your entries. Deleting the app deletes this data.

The CSV export in Settings leaves your device only when you choose to share it, through the iOS share sheet, to a destination you pick.

What Sundry itself collects

Nothing. The app contains no analytics and sends no data of its own to the developer or to anyone else.

Advertising

Sundry shows banner ads served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device identifiers, the ads you have seen or tapped, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Sundry does not ask to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. The app never requests the App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use your device’s advertising identifier for cross-app tracking.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Sundry asks for your consent before serving personalised ads, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.

You can also limit ad tracking on your device at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Children

Sundry is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.
