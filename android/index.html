Sundry for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026

Sundry is an expense tracker for Android, made by Maxim Cucerenco. This policy explains what the app does — and does not do — with your data. (Using an iPhone? The iOS policy is here.)

Your entries stay on your device

Everything you record in Sundry — amounts, categories, notes and dates — is stored only in the app’s local database on your phone. Sundry has no accounts, no sign-in and no server of its own, so the developer cannot see your entries. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Accounts inside Sundry (“Personal”, “Business”) are labels for separating your own records. They are not logins and protect nothing: anyone holding the unlocked phone can open every one of them.

The CSV export in Settings leaves your device only when you choose to share it, through the Android share sheet, to a destination you pick.

If you have Android’s backup enabled, your ledger may be included in your device’s backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms. Your ad consent choice is deliberately excluded from that backup.

What Sundry itself collects

Nothing. The app contains no analytics and sends no data of its own to the developer or to anyone else.

Advertising

Sundry shows banner ads served by Google AdMob (the Google Mobile Ads SDK). To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as your IP address (used to estimate an approximate location), device identifiers including the advertising ID, the ads you have seen or tapped, and crash and performance diagnostics. Google describes this in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

Your expenses are never part of this. They stay on your phone and are never shared with Google or anyone else.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Sundry asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google’s User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options inside the app.

Wherever you are, you can limit ad personalisation on your device in Settings → Privacy → Ads, where Android also lets you delete or reset your advertising ID. Deleting the advertising ID stops it being available to apps, including this one.

Your privacy rights in the United States

Residents of California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas and the other US states with consumer privacy laws have the right to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of their personal information, including its use for targeted advertising. Sundry itself collects nothing about you and sells nothing; the only data this can apply to is what Google collects to show ads, described above.

If you live in one of these states, you can opt out at any time from Settings → Ad privacy options in the app. Once you do, Google limits how it uses your data and Sundry shows only non-personalised ads. You can also delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone’s Settings → Privacy → Ads.

You can also ask what information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to the address below. We will never treat you differently for using these rights.

Tips

Tipping is optional and goes entirely through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy; Sundry never sees your payment details. On your device the app only records that a tip went through, so it can hide its own banners for three days. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

Watching the optional rewarded ad in Support Us is the same Google AdMob service described above: it plays only when you tap it, and its only effect is hiding the banners for four hours.

Children

Sundry is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Data deletion

Because your entries never leave your device, there is no account to close and nothing held on a server to erase. To delete everything Sundry has stored, delete individual entries in the app, or uninstall the app.
